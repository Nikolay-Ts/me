An Android app that decides what you should eat, built because I am hopeless at
picking a meal. It suggests food three ways: completely at random, by category and
dietary restriction, or from whatever ingredients you already have in your fridge.

The home screen shows a "daily meal" that reshuffles every day, and each recipe
view carries the full instructions plus an embedded YouTube video. The fridge keeps
an inventory you can multi-select from — long-press to pick several ingredients and
search a meal built out of them, or search with nothing selected to get something
random.
